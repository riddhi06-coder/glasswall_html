Architectural Louvers

Architectural louvers are fixed or operable slatted systems installed on a building façade to provide ventilation, solar shading, weather protection, and aesthetic enhancement. They are commonly manufactured from aluminium, making them lightweight, durable, and resistant to corrosion.

Architectural louvers consist of angled blades mounted within a supporting frame. They allow air to flow through the building while reducing the entry of rain, dust, and direct sunlight. Besides their functional role, louvers contribute to the building's architectural identity by creating distinctive façade patterns.
