Message from the Managing Director

At Glass Wall System, sustainability is a fundamental pillar of our corporate philosophy and operational strategy. We are dedicated to engineering façade solutions that exemplify excellence in design while upholding the highest standard of environmental responsibility through the use of carbon neutral materials, energy-efficient processes, and sustainable sourcing practices. We proactively optimize our product to minimize material waste at the design stage, ensuring efficient use of resources and promoting circularity throughout the product lifecycle. As we pursue ambitious targets to reduce our carbon footprint and contribute positively to society, we remain steadfast in our commitment to driving meaningful progress towards a more sustainable and resilient built environment.

Mr. Eshan Hemrajani

- Managing Director and CEO

Our ESG Priorities

Where innovation sustainability, andcommunity meet

01

Environment

We work to minimise our environmental impact through responsible material sourcing, waste reduction and renewable energy, with around 70% of our factory's electricity drawn from on-site solar installed capacity. Our façades are engineered for low-embodied carbon.

02

People and Community

Our people and communities are at the heart of our sustainability journey. We invest in a safe, inclusive and growth-driven workplace, while contributing to the wellbeing of the communities we operate in through meaningful CSR initiatives.

03

Governance

We uphold strong governance practices rooted in ethics, transparency and accountability. Our operations are guided by clear internal standards and policies, regulatory compliance and responsible sourcing, ensuring trust and long-term value for all stakeholders.

04

Measurement and Assurance

We believe impact should be measured, not assumed. Five third-party verified EPDs, Scope 1, 2 and 3 accounting and project-level carbon certificates support our clients' LEED, IGBC and GRIHA submissions.

Sustainablity Goals

Our Alignment with UN Sustainable Development Goals

At Glass Wall System, our sustainability strategy is built around key United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), including clean water and sanitation, affordable and clean energy, innovation and infrastructure, sustainable cities, responsible production, and global partnerships. We focus on reducing environmental impact, using renewable energy, promoting water efficiency, and supporting circular design practices. Through innovation, collaboration, and responsible resource use, we aim to contribute to a more sustainable, inclusive, and resilient future for all.

Stakeholder

Our Stakeholders

At Glass Wall Systems, we believe meaningful progress happens through strong stakeholder collaboration. From clients and employees to suppliers, communities, and regulators – we actively engage each group to align their expectations with our sustainability goals. This shared commitment helps us create façades that reflects not only design excellence but also environmental and social responsibility.

Waste and Circularity

At Glass Wall System, waste is not an endpoint – it’s a resource. We adopt a proactive approach to reduce, reuse, and recycle materials across our operations, aligning with zero waste to landfill and circular economy principles.

Zero Waste to Landfill

All production waste is diverted to certified recycling partners.

Circular Economy Integration

Materials like aluminium and silicone are reintegrated into the supply chain.

Design for Material Efficiency

Façades are engineered to reduce scrap at the design stage.

Third-Party Waste Management

Authorized vendors handle and recycle all process waste responsibly.
