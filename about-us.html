About Us

Building future architectural marvels, today.

Glass Wall Systems (India) Limited is a leading turnkey façade specialist, delivering integrated design, engineering, manufacturing and installation for commercial, residential and institutional projects. We seamlessly combine in-house design, precision manufacturing and expert on-site execution to build durable, high-performance building façades.

From high-performing unitized curtain walls and structural glazing to custom architectural metalwork, aluminium fabrication and ACP cladding, we deliver durable, project-specific solutions from concept to installation.

We are headquartered in Mumbai, with a strong business presence in Bangalore, Delhi, NCR, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Hyderabad and Pune making us a true Pan India company. We have also expanded our operations and expertise to USA, Australia, Qatar and Sri Lanka, where we offer a full range of glazing systems and custom-designed architectural metal works.
