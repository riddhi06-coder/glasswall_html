Diagrid

The egg-shaped diagrid is formed with M.S. circular pipes. The façade system was specially designed in a cascaded form to cater to 3-directional movement. Each panel was fabricated to an accuracy of 0.5 mm to suit the 3-dimensional variation on site.

Free Flowing Lobby

The lobby is a triple-height structure, and the free-flowing space is enclosed with a mesh of rectangular tubes (diagrid), which is glazed with laminated double-glass units. The 2,000 sq. m. diagrid façade is self-supporting, with no structural member supporting the façade. The steel trusses were fabricated in segments of up to 12 m in length.

Curved Unitized Panels

Cold profile bending was used for all members, including coupling, gutter, and sill profiles. Radii as small as 1.55 m to 0.9 m were achieved. 6063-T6 aluminium temper was used. System width: 110 mm.

Articulated Unitized Panels

The unitized system is designed to accommodate glass in different planes and slant profiles within a single unitized panel. The system can withstand wind pressure of up to 5 kPa. The articulated design of the system creates the effect of a sparkling water or diamond surface.

Dynamic Façade Lighting

The diamond edges of the building feature aluminium flashing with integrated LED lighting. The façade incorporates LED lighting within the spandrel panels, creating a dynamic appearance at night. Media façade. Double-skin façade. Aluminium fins incorporated with integrated lighting provisions.

Curved ACP Panel with System

Curved ACP panels measuring 6 m x 1.55 m and weighing more than 200 kg. Segmented aluminium support system. Beam panels manufactured to the required degree of curvature at 1.1.

Curved Aluminium Sheet

3 mm thick aluminium bent sheets. PVDF coated for enhanced durability and weather resistance. Aluminium runners attached to the civil structure.

Perforated Aluminium Screen

22 die-hole sizes incorporated into a single panel, with a perforation percentage of 80%. Panel dimensions: 4.5 m x 1.2 m. Inclined bottom cap. Total façade area: 1,600 sq. m.

Free Flowing Façade

3-dimensional designing of each member and glass to achieve the dynamic shape intended. Pre-fabricated members based on the geometrical model delivered from factory and erected with precession at site.
