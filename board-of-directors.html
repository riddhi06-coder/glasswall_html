GWS is led by dynamic and experienced promoters, assisted by a dedicated and talented team of over 330 personnel, comprising of highly skilled CAs and MBAs, and engineers with designing, mechanical and execution expertise.

01

Jawahar H. Hemrajani

Chairman and Director

After successfully completing his degree in Law and Commerce, Jawahar H. Hemrajani took up a modest 9-to-5 job but always yearned for something more fulfilling. He eventually set up a facility to coat powder on metal in Mumbai, India. After establishing one successful business, he decided to conquer new horizons by setting up Glass Wall Systems.

Having been in the façade business for over 33 years, Jawahar has a keen understanding of the technical intricacies and how one can push boundaries to create new architectural wonders. His entrepreneurial knowledge and expertise contribute largely to all projects, in particular when dealing with advanced and complex façades to ensure the client’s highest expectations are being meticulously achieved by GWS.

Jawahar has been spearheading the company’s current journey towards global expansion by seizing geographical opportunities. At Glass Wall Systems, he is looking forward to bringing his rich experience to global customers.

02

Eshan J. Hemrajani

Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer

Eshan J. Hemrajani joined Glass Wall Systems in the year 2014 and looks after Operations, Human Resources, Marketing, and overall factory operations. He has completed his MSc. in Engineering Business Management from Warwick University, UK, and also has a B.E. Civil Degree from Cardiff University, UK.

At GWS, Eshan focuses on leveraging his invaluable experience on the operations side of business, to deliver products as per the required international standards, within the demanding delivery timelines. Eshan aims to strategically position GWS firmly on the global map, by focusing on turnkey international projects. He is also steering the development and implementation of the ‘Green Initiative’ drive for sustainable façades manufacturing within the company.

03

Amit J. Hemrajani

Director

Amit J. Hemrajani is a graduate in Architecture from the Royal Institute of British Architects (UK), with over 9 years of hands-on experience in the façade and fenestration industry. Under his leadership, the company has successfully completed more than 100 high-end residential projects for clients across India.

At Glass Wall Systems, he brings deep technical expertise in powder coating, sheet metal and window fabrication. Currently, he oversees sales and operations for the company’s window projects across global markets, driving innovation and excellence in every project.
